Longbow and voice

Bodies born with no mana can now find the Empty longbow on the trial road: a full draw flies flat and passes through foes, and R picks the arrow (reed, iron, ember or ink). Spells on your keys and the chants you carry are now chosen at shrines, and a body with no mana carries five chants instead of three. Check your mic before you play: the game listens to you say three spells and learns how your voice comes through, and it keeps learning in play when it mishears you and you press the key instead. Starting a chant over no longer spoils it.
