The chart's way

Your chart sets how much mana a body has, which school it belongs to, and its way of play. Press Tab and open the Body page to read why, in plain words. Each school now has its own signature. Mana grows a little with each deep stillness and swells under a real full Moon. A Sovereign body is stronger alone: more damage, one more prana, and it feels foes beyond the edge of sight. A Sangha body is stronger with others beside it (for now, the beings you call and the shade of a past life). A Balanced body has a little of both.

From build 20:

Five roads lead toward realization: knowing, love, right action, stillness, and the road walked alone. You walk them by what you do, never by what you say, and no number tells you how far you are. A shrine only lets you feel the roads under your feet. Each road has three steps, each step gives a small gift, and no road ends in a single life. A teacher sits by the pool shrine. Bowing to them opens the road of love, and closes the road walked alone for this soul, for good. A body born with no mana that walks the road of right action learns body arts no caster ever gets.

The mic check asks you to say three spells and learns how your voice comes through.
